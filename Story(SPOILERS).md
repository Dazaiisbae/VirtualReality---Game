Memory 001: (inspired by movie "In Time")

Photo 1: Billboard - In this city, your life was currency. Rent, food, and rides were paid in years. Most people were broke by thirty. The top one percent never ran out. The billboard says: SPEND YOUR TIME WISELY. The Archiver notes that this was not advice.

Photo 2: Street - Nobody here walks slowly. Walking slowly is expensive. Look at their wrists, every one of them is counting down.

Photo 3: Lit Window - One child, asleep, with a full lifetime still unspent. Their parents paid for it. The Archiver checked the receipts. They paid with everything they had. You don’t have to say anything, Just keep the picture.
